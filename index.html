Got Questions?

Parents Often Ask...

Find immediate, transparent guidance on admissions, daily schedules, prospectus downloads, and institutional facilities.

Admissions at Smt. Annapurna C. Hiremath School follow a transparent, child-friendly 5-step pathway:

  1. Initial Enquiry: Submit an enquiry online or visit our administrative reception.
  2. Application Submission: Complete the registration form with the child's birth certificate/transfer certificate and Aadhaar copy.
  3. Interactive Session: An informal, encouraging conversation with the child and parents to understand learning readiness.
  4. Document Verification: Review of previous school records and address verification.
  5. Seat Confirmation: Fee payment and allotment of House, uniform sizing, and section.
