State-of-the-art learning environments designed to stimulate creativity, scientific inquiry, and student well-being.

Experiential Science

Composite Science Laboratory

Fully equipped laboratory supporting hands-on Physics, Chemistry, and Biology experiments with safety apparatus, compound microscopes, test racks, and real specimen sets.

Digital Technology

Modern Computer & ICT Lab

High-speed networked computer terminals providing foundational digital literacy, typing, office productivity, safe internet browsing, and introductory coding exercises.

Knowledge Center

Resource & Reference Library

Quiet sanctuary housing thousands of books spanning English classics, regional Kannada literature, encyclopedias, children's storybooks, and competitive exam guides.

Interactive Smart Classrooms

Expansive outdoor grounds hosting morning assemblies, athletics sprint tracks, volleyball, kho-kho courts, and kabaddi arenas under certified PE supervision.

Punctual and safe student bus transportation connecting residential areas of Alnavar and surrounding villages with experienced drivers and on-board attendants.
