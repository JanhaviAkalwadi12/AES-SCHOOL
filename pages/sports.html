Physical Vitality & Team Spirit

Sports & Athletics

Building endurance, sportsmanship, and leadership on the field and beyond.

Sports Philosophy

A Sound Mind in a Healthy Body

Physical education is an indispensable pillar of our educational ethos. Under the direction of qualified Physical Education instructors, every student participates in structured athletic training, team sports, and yoga drills.

From regular physical training (PT) assemblies to high-intensity inter-house tournaments and taluk/district level championships, we instill grit, fair play, and lifelong fitness.

Sports Disciplines

Sports & Games Offered

Wide range of outdoor athletics and indoor strategy games.

🏃

Track & Field Athletics

100m, 200m, 400m sprint events, 4x100m relay, long jump, high jump, shot put, and endurance running.

🏐

Volleyball & Throwball

Dedicated outdoor courts with regular house league matches, serving drills, spiking practice, and inter-school tournaments.

🤼

Kho-Kho & Kabaddi

Traditional Indian indigenous sports building remarkable agility, lung power, team strategy, and quick reflexes.

🏸

Badminton

Outdoor and indoor badminton practice courts focusing on footwork, hand-eye coordination, and singles/doubles technique.

♟️

Chess & Carrom (Indoor)

Strategic board games developing intense focus, forward anticipation, concentration, and patient analytical thinking.

🧘

Yoga & Mass PT Drills

Daily morning yoga asanas, deep breathing pranayama, marching band coordination, and synchronized mass drills.
