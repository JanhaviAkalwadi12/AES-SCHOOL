Daily Routine

A Day at Our School

A balanced daily schedule ensuring intellectual stimulation, physical vitality, and moral reflection.

08:00 AM

Campus Arrival & Welcoming

School buses arrive; students enter through monitored gates and greet mentors.

08:15 AM – 08:45 AM

Morning Assembly & Value Thought

School prayer, state anthem, thought for the day, news reading, and physical drill in house formation.

09:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Core Academic Instruction

High-focus morning academic blocks covering Mathematics, Science, and Languages.

11:00 AM – 11:20 AM

Nutritional Break

Recharging with healthy snacks and social relaxation with classmates.

11:20 AM – 01:00 PM

Laboratory & ICT Sessions

Hands-on practicals in Science Labs, Computer Lab, and interactive smart board modules.

01:00 PM – 01:45 PM

Lunch Hour & Open Play

Wholesome lunch under faculty supervision and supervised ground recreation.

01:45 PM – 03:30 PM

Co-Curriculars, Arts & Sports

Physical education drills, volleyball, kho-kho, library reading, fine arts, and ATL innovation projects.

03:45 PM

Orderly Dismissal & Bus Transit

Closing anthem, diary checks, and escorted boarding onto school transport.

Student Democracy

The Student Parliament

Our elected Student Council provides practical experience in democratic leadership, institutional responsibility, event planning, and peer mediation.

Cabinet positions include the Prime Minister, Discipline Minister, Environmental Minister, Sports Minister, Cultural Minister, Finance Minister, Excursion Minister, and House Captains for Red, Yellow, Blue, and Green Houses.

STEM & 21st Century Skills

ATL Innovation & Robotics Lab

"IMAGINE. CREATE. INNOVATE."

Robotics & Sensor Kits

Assembling programmable motorized robots, infrared obstacle detectors, line-following buggies, and servo mechanisms.

Coding & Computational Logic

Visual block coding, algorithmic thinking, Python fundamentals, and building interactive educational mini-games.

Electronics & Circuit Prototyping

Hands-on breadboard circuit assembly, microcontrollers (Arduino), LEDs, solar cells, and automated irrigation prototypes.
