Our Roots

Alnavar Education Society's Legacy

A journey of educational service dedicated to uplifting the community through accessible quality learning.

FOUNDATION ERA

Visionary Founding of the Society

The Alnavar Education Society was registered by community leaders with the noble objective of establishing a premier educational institution in Alnavar.

GROWTH & INCEPTION

Establishment of English Medium School

Founded in honour of Smt. Annapurna C. Hiremath, providing quality primary education with an English-first pedagogical curriculum.

EXPANSION

High School Section & Laboratory Wings

Expanded to comprehensive high school education with state-of-the-art science laboratories, digital learning tools, and sports arenas.

PRESENT DAY

Modernized Multi-Wing Campus

Today, educating over 1200+ students with 100% SSLC board achievements, dynamic student council governance, and ATL STEM innovation.
