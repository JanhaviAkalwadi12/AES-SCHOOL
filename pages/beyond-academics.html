House Camaraderie

The Four School Houses

Every student belongs to a vibrant House that fosters healthy competition, camaraderie, and team pride.

RED HOUSE
Motto: Courage & Valour

Instilling fierce resilience, sportsmanship, and brave pursuit of excellence in all inter-house track, field, and debate contests.

YELLOW HOUSE
Motto: Wisdom & Radiance

Celebrating intellectual sharpness, quiz championships, creative arts, and illuminating fellow students through scholarly guidance.

BLUE HOUSE
Motto: Integrity & Loyalty

Championing fair play, unflinching institutional discipline, team harmony, and stellar performance in cultural festivals.

GREEN HOUSE
Motto: Growth & Harmony

Leading eco-stewardship, community cleanliness drives, sustainable innovation, and continuous personal growth.

Student Clubs

Co-Curricular Clubs

Weekly club periods allowing every child to cultivate lifelong creative, scientific, and cultural skills.

Literary & Debating Society

English, Kannada, and Hindi elocution, extempore speech, poetry recitation, essay writing, and inter-school debate championships.

Science & Young Inventors Club

Practical experiments, model building, telescope stargazing sessions, science quiz competitions, and district expo participation.

Cultural Arts & Dramatics

Folk dance, Bharatanatyam, light classical music, patriotic choral singing, and stage theatricals for school celebrations.

Eco Club & Environmental Ministry

Campus afforestation, sapling distribution, plastic-free drives, herbal garden maintenance, and energy conservation awareness.

Fine Arts & Origami Studio

Drawing, sketching, clay sculpture, traditional rangoli, canvas acrylics, and design for school exhibitions and newsletters.

Yoga & Wellness Circle

Daily morning pranayama, Surya Namaskar, mindful concentration techniques, and physical flexibility training for mental clarity.

Experiential Learning

Educational Excursions & Field Tours

Learning comes alive when students step outside the classroom. Annual guided excursions and local industrial, botanical, and historical visits provide unforgettable experiential insights.

Students visit historical forts of Karnataka, agricultural research institutes, planetariums, power plants, and nature sanctuaries under strict faculty supervision.
